THE DECLINING GIANT

Consumer Electronics

Retail

Established in 2008, the consumer electronics company sells smartphones, laptops, tablets, and other electronic devices. It became a leading brand by offering affordable, reliable products through a wide distribution network.

STOCK PRICE$80 → $52
01 / THE SITUATION

The problem

Sales and profitability have declined over the past three years. Competitors offer cheaper products and newer technologies, causing customers to switch and the consumer electronics company’s market share to fall.

02 / WHO IS AFFECTED

Stakeholders

  • Customers are shifting to competitors.
  • Shareholders are worried about continued decline.
  • Competitors are expanding their market position.
  • Analysts are questioning the consumer electronics company’s ability to compete.
ROOT CAUSES

What is driving the problem?

  • Outdated products
  • Strong price competition
  • Weak marketing to younger consumers
  • Poor customer retention

THE FINANCIAL TRAP

Automotive Manufacturing

Electric Vehicles

the automotive company is an established automotive manufacturer focused on affordable passenger vehicles. It has held a strong domestic position for more than 20 years and is now moving into electric vehicles (EVs).

STOCK PRICE$95 → $61
01 / THE SITUATION

The problem

The company borrowed $95M to fund an EV factory, equipment, battery technology, and research and development. Construction delays and equipment shortages increased costs and borrowing expenses. EV demand has been weaker than projected because of competition and pricing, making it difficult to manage debt while maintaining operations.

02 / WHO IS AFFECTED

Stakeholders

  • Investors are worried about repayment.
  • Banks are cautious about further lending.
  • EV competitors are gaining market share.
  • Customers are concerned about vehicle prices and charging infrastructure.
ROOT CAUSES

What is driving the problem?

  • Excessive borrowing
  • Unexpected project expenses
  • Weaker-than-expected EV demand
  • Higher interest expenses

Supply Chain Services

the logistics company provides logistics services to retailers, manufacturers, and e-commerce businesses. It operates in several countries, has a large fleet and distribution network, and maintains relationships with major retailers.

The problem

  • Employees are concerned about possible job changes.

What is driving the problem?
